# Defect bank for seeded calibration passages

Each seeded passage gets exactly one defect from this bank, chosen to fit the passage naturally (same topic, grammatical in context). Record the category and the exact injected span. Typography defects are excluded: they are lint's job, not the readers'.

## nl (Dutch)

Calques and wrong collocations (confirmed in a native read, 2026-11-17):
- "lenen zich beter om op te warmen" (for: kun je beter opwarmen)
- "laten zich op hun best zien" (for: zijn het lekkerst)
- "past naast" (for: past bij)
- "Er is geen haast om ... te" (for: ... hoeft niet ...)
- "Dat maakt niet elke X een Y" (for: Dat betekent niet dat elke X ...)
- "het resultaat is diepgang" style English cleft: "is waar de meeste ... op gebouwd zijn"
- "maakt sense" / "maakt het verschil"
- "aan het einde van de dag" (for: uiteindelijk)
- "in een notendop" as a section closer
AI tells (from locales/nl.md and anti-slop.md):
- "Ontdek de wereld van ..." as an opener
- "Duik in ..." / "Stap in de wereld van ..."
- "niet alleen ..., maar ook ..." (staged contrast)
- "naadloos", "cruciaal", "essentieel" used as praise
- a rhythmic triplet of adjectives: "fris, verrassend en verfijnd"
- a closing one-line punchline after a full paragraph: "Proef het verschil."
Owner-confirmed (owner check, 2026-11-18):
- a bare ratio in consumer copy: "smaken het best als highball 1:2" (for: één deel yuzusake op twee delen soda)
Register slips:
- one "u" or "uw" in a passage that otherwise uses "je/jij" (or the reverse)

## en (English)

Calques and translationese:
- "on the end" (for: in the end); "make a photo" (for: take a photo)
- "Dutch-style" word order: "Tomorrow come we by" type inversions, kept subtle: "Then can you choose ..."
- "actual" for "current" ("the actual price"); "eventually" for "possibly"
AI tells:
- "Dive into ...", "Unlock the power of ...", "Elevate your ..."
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "delve"
- tricolon of adjectives; a one-line punchline closer
Register slips:
- a sudden formal "One should ..." or "Kindly ..." in a casual passage
