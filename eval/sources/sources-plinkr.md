## 1. What Plinkr is

- Tagline: "Iedereen van schuld naar vrij." (W/home.txt). Social enterprise, offices Eindhoven and Amsterdam, info@plinkr.nl, 085 - 130 16 37, "Plinkr is ISO 27001 gecertificeerd" (W/home.txt footer).
- Plinkr Platform: "Het Plinkr Platform helpt gemeenten en hulporganisaties om de financiële keten slimmer te organiseren. Zodat meer inwoners op tijd geholpen worden." Two surfaces: "De platform omgeving voor hulpverleners" and "De platform app voor inwoners" (W/homeplatform.txt). Residents get "gratis toegang tot een eigen, digitale Plinkr omgeving" (W/homeplatform.txt step 4).
- Plinkr Nazorg: method for "coaching en nazorg" after/around debt help, with digital "redzaamheidstrajecten", an app for residents, a coach chosen by the municipality, measuring tools; modules for schuldhulpverlening en budgetbeheer, financiële hulpverlening, Bewind; "geaccrediteerd door SchuldenLabNL"; VNG handreiking (W/homenazorg.txt).
- FHC, De Financiële Hulpketen Check: questionnaire sent to organisations in a municipality, answers anonymous, results in a report and presentation (R/marketing-site/src/pages/fhc.js). Not a resident product.
- ffc (R/ffc) = "Financieel Fit Check", a multi-tenant triage tool. Resident-facing brand name in mails is "Plinkr Wegwijzer" / "Wegwijzer". Signallers (toeleiders) fill it in together with a resident; a decision tree routes to an organisation; emails go to referrer, resident, organisation, volunteer org (R/ffc/README.md, R/ffc/docs/prd.md, R/ffc/lambdas/send-email/index.ts). Active municipalities per PRD: Zaanstad, Alphen aan den Rijn, Kampen; planned Eindhoven, Westerkwartier (R/ffc/docs/prd.md, may be stale).
- portal (R/portal/README.md): "A web application for Plinkr coaches." Dutch only.
- mijnplinkr (R/mijnplinkr): React Router/Cloudflare web app; its i18n namespaces ("web-client", "client-onboarding", "budget-tips", "categories") point to the resident web client (R/mijnplinkr/node_scripts/fetchTranslations.js). The locales folder in the repo is empty. Audience is an inference.
- platform-mobile (R/platform-mobile/README.md): "Plinkr Mobile Platform", Expo app. Its language file is empty: R/platform-mobile/languages/english.json contains only `{}`. Resident audience is inferred (it is the "Plinkr App" in the Wegwijzer mails).
- Languages: Dutch only. mijnplinkr supportedLngs ["nl"] (R/mijnplinkr/app/lib/i18n.ts); portal supportedLngs ['nl'] (R/portal/src/i18n.js); english.json is empty. **No English copy exists.** Any English eval task is invented, not sourced.
- Register: resident-facing = je/jij (TR client-onboarding; Wegwijzer resident mail). Coach/portal = je (TR default, "Je hebt geen openstaande uitnodigingen"). Marketing site toward municipalities = je/jouw ("in jouw gemeente", W/home.txt). Organisations in Wegwijzer mails = u/uw ("naar uw organisatie doorverwezen", R/ffc/lambdas/send-email/index.ts). Signaller-facing UI in ffc: "Vul de benodigde gegevens aan." (imperative, no pronoun).
- Customers/quotes: Assen (Jaap Gernaat, Beleidsadviseur Sociaal Domein), Zaanstad newspaper article (W/homeplatform.txt), 2020-2023 Impact Update (W/homenazorg.txt).
