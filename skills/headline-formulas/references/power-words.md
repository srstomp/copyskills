# Power Words

Words categorized by the emotional response they trigger. These are the words professional copywriters actually reach for, not academic lists.

**Usage rule:** 1-2 power words per headline maximum. Never stack two from the same category. Stacking kills credibility and reads as hype.

---

## Urgency

Force action now. Best for CTAs, limited-time offers, and deadline-driven subject lines.

- Now
- Today
- Immediately
- Expires
- Deadline
- Last chance
- Before it's gone
- Running out
- Final
- Closing
- Act fast
- Don't wait
- Hours left
- Ends tonight
- Limited time
- While supplies last
- This week only

---

## Exclusivity

Signal that not everyone gets this. Works for invite-only, early access, member content, and premium offers.

- Exclusive
- Private
- Members-only
- Invitation only
- Insider
- Behind closed doors
- Off-limits
- Restricted
- Confidential
- By application
- Selected
- Vetted
- Inner circle
- Not public
- First access
- Early
- Reserved

---

## Trust

Reduce friction and lower perceived risk. Essential in guarantees, testimonials, pricing pages, and objection-handling copy.

- Proven
- Guaranteed
- Verified
- Tested
- Trusted
- Backed
- Certified
- Documented
- Results
- Track record
- Risk-free
- No-strings
- Refund
- Money-back
- Transparent
- Independent
- Peer-reviewed

---

## Curiosity

Create an information gap the reader must close. Use in subject lines, blog titles, and social hooks.

- Secret
- Hidden
- Unknown
- Surprising
- Counterintuitive
- Overlooked
- Rarely discussed
- The real reason
- What they won't tell you
- Behind the scenes
- Unpublished
- Leaked
- Discovered
- Confession
- What actually happens
- The truth about

---

## Fear / Loss

Activate loss aversion. Use carefully in pain-point copy, re-engagement, and warning-style subject lines. Don't manufacture fear that isn't real.

- Losing
- Risk
- Danger
- Costly
- Mistake
- Warning
- Fail
- Miss out
- Left behind
- Falling behind
- Threat
- Penalty
- Gap

---

## Desire / Gain

Paint the picture of the upside. Works across all copy types, especially benefit headlines, case studies, and aspirational hooks.

- Win
- Gain
- Grow
- Profit
- Advantage
- Edge
- Boost
- Accelerate
- Multiply
- Achieve
- Finally
- Results

---

## Usage Examples

Words in context perform differently than words in lists. A few examples of the same headline with and without a power word:

| Without | With | Category used |
|---------|------|---------------|
| "Get more replies" | "Finally get more replies" | Desire/gain |
| "A/B test your subject lines" | "Secretly A/B test your subject lines" | Curiosity |
| "Last week to apply" | "Final week to apply" | Urgency |
| "Our process" | "Our proven process" | Trust |
| "Don't miss this" | "Don't risk missing this" | Fear/loss |
