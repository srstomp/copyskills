# Platform Specs

Character limits, image specs, hashtag conventions, and algorithm preferences for each major platform. Load this file in Step 2 before drafting any social copy.

---

## X / Twitter

### Character Limits
- Tweet: 280 characters (including spaces, punctuation, and newlines)
- URLs: always count as 23 characters regardless of actual length
- Images, videos, and polls do not consume characters
- Thread: 280 characters per tweet, no cap on number of tweets
- Bio: 160 characters
- Display name: 50 characters

### Image and Video Specs
- In-feed image: 1600 x 900 px recommended (16:9), minimum 600 x 335 px
- Square image: 1080 x 1080 px
- Max image file size: 5 MB (PNG/JPEG), 15 MB (GIF)
- Video: up to 2 minutes 20 seconds, MP4 or MOV, max 512 MB
- Video dimensions: 1280 x 720 px recommended (landscape), 720 x 1280 px (portrait)
- Aspect ratio range: 1:2.39 to 2.39:1

### Hashtag Conventions
- 1-2 hashtags maximum for organic reach
- More than 2 hashtags measurably reduces engagement
- Trending hashtags inside the tweet body outperform appended tags
- No hashtag is often the right call for threads (topic is the hook, not the tag)

### Algorithm Preferences (2025-2026)
- Rewards engagement velocity: replies, retweets, and quote posts in the first 30 minutes matter most
- Long replies and quote posts signal quality to the algorithm
- External links in tweet body may be downranked in feed; link in reply is a common workaround
- Original content outperforms retweets in reach
- Threads outperform single tweets in impressions if engagement on tweet 1 is strong
- Subscriber/premium accounts may receive additional reach boosts

## LinkedIn

### Character Limits
- Post: 3000 characters
- Article preview / introductory text: 700 characters
- Headline: 220 characters
- Summary (About): 2600 characters
- Comment: 1250 characters
- Connection request note: 300 characters

### Image and Video Specs
- Single image: 1200 x 627 px recommended (1.91:1), or 1200 x 1200 px (square)
- Carousel (document post): PDF or PPTX, 1080 x 1080 px per slide or 1920 x 1080 px
- Video: 256 x 144 px minimum, 4096 x 2304 px maximum, 3 seconds to 30 minutes, max 5 GB
- Video aspect ratios: 1:2.4 to 2.4:1

### Hashtag Conventions
- 3-5 hashtags is the standard range for organic posts
- More than 5 hashtags is often associated with lower-quality content in the algorithm
- Place hashtags at the end of the post, after the CTA
- Mix broad tags (e.g., #marketing) with specific tags (e.g., #b2bmarketing)
- Never embed hashtags inside sentences on LinkedIn

### Algorithm Preferences (2025-2026)
- Rewards dwell time: how long someone pauses on your post matters more than passive impressions
- Comments (especially longer ones) are the highest-value signal
- Reactions rank: "Insightful" and "Support" may outrank generic "Like" in feed weighting
- The first 60-90 minutes after posting determine distribution window
- Text-only posts with line breaks often match or outperform image posts
- Polls generate comments but may limit distribution to engaged followers
- Avoid posting links in the post body; put them in the first comment for better reach
- Personal profiles consistently outperform Company Pages in organic reach
- Native documents (carousels uploaded as PDFs) outperform image carousels

## Instagram

### Character Limits
- Caption: 2200 characters
- Visible before "more" truncation: roughly 125 characters (about 2-3 lines)
- Bio: 150 characters
- Comment: 2200 characters

### Image and Video Specs
- Square image: 1080 x 1080 px (1:1) -- most reliable format
- Portrait image: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5) -- takes more screen real estate in feed
- Landscape image: 1080 x 566 px (1.91:1)
- Reel: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16), up to 15 minutes, MP4 or MOV
- Carousel: up to 20 images or videos per post, 1080 x 1080 px recommended
- Stories: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16)

### Hashtag Conventions
- Maximum: 30 hashtags per post
- Effective range: 5-10 hashtags with strong niche relevance
- Hashtag stuffing (20+) no longer drives meaningful additional reach
- Place hashtags at the end of the caption or in the first comment (either works)
- Mix: 1-2 broad tags, 3-5 mid-size niche tags, 1-2 very specific micro-niche tags
- Stories: up to 10 hashtags, but 1-3 targeted hashtags is more common

### Algorithm Preferences (2025-2026)
- Saves and shares are the highest-value engagement signals (they indicate content worth returning to or distributing)
- Comments matter more than likes
- Reels receive preferential feed distribution over static image posts
- Carousel posts get second-chance distribution: the algorithm re-shows the post to users who swiped through
- Posting consistency (regular cadence) matters more than posting frequency
- Close Friends and broadcast channels signal strong community, which increases reach
- Stories keep you top-of-mind between feed posts but have separate algorithmic logic
- Hashtags provide less discovery value than in prior years; keyword-rich captions now matter more for SEO within the app

## TikTok

### Character Limits
- Video description / caption: 2200 characters
- Visible before truncation in feed: roughly 100-150 characters
- Bio: 80 characters
- Comment: 150 characters

### Image and Video Specs
- Video: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16) strongly recommended, 1:1 and 16:9 also supported
- Max video length: 10 minutes (some accounts up to 60 minutes)
- Minimum video length: 3 seconds (but 15-60 seconds performs best for most content)
- File size max: 287.6 MB
- Photo carousel (TikTok Photos): up to 35 images per post

### Hashtag Conventions
- 3-5 hashtags is the effective range
- Combine: 1 broad trending tag, 1-2 niche-specific tags, 1 hyper-specific community tag
- Trend-based hashtags drive short-term visibility; niche tags drive sustained views
- Do not pad with generic tags (#fyp, #foryou): they signal low effort

### Algorithm Preferences (2025-2026)
- Watch time and completion rate are the primary ranking signals
- Re-watches (viewer watching again) indicate strong content and trigger wider distribution
- Comments and shares matter more than likes
- Hook quality in first 1-3 seconds determines whether the video gets pushed to a wider audience
- Caption hook supports the video hook -- viewers who read the caption before pressing play are primed
- Posting at consistent times builds audience expectation but algorithm reach depends on content quality above all
- Longer videos (3-7 minutes) receive boosted distribution in 2025 as TikTok competes with YouTube
- Search is increasingly how users find TikTok content: keyword-rich captions improve discoverability
