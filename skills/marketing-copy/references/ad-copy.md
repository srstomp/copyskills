# Ad Copy Reference

Character limits, format specs, and structural patterns for Google Search, Meta/Facebook, and LinkedIn ads. Check limits here before drafting a single character.

---

## Google Search Ads

### Character limits

| Field | Limit | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Headline | 30 characters each | Up to 15 headlines; Google shows 2-3 at a time |
| Description | 90 characters each | Up to 4 descriptions; Google shows up to 2 at a time |
| Display URL path | 15 characters each | Two optional path fields appended to domain |
| Final URL | No character limit | Must match the landing page domain |

Google may pin specific headlines to positions 1, 2, or 3. Write each headline to stand alone -- they will appear in combinations you don't control.

### Responsive Search Ad structure

```
Headline 1: [30 chars] -- Primary value claim or keyword match
Headline 2: [30 chars] -- Differentiator or secondary benefit
Headline 3: [30 chars] -- CTA or social proof hook
...up to 15 headlines total

Description 1: [90 chars] -- Expand the headline with specifics and CTA
Description 2: [90 chars] -- Handle an objection or add urgency + CTA

Display URL: yourdomain.com/[path1]/[path2]
```

### Writing patterns for search ads

Search ads match intent. The reader typed a query. Your job is to be the most relevant result for what they were looking for.

**Intent-matching:** Mirror the searcher's language. If they searched "crm for small business," your headline should contain those words or close synonyms.

**Benefit-first structure:** Lead with the outcome, not the feature.
- Weak: "Advanced CRM With Pipeline Management"
- Strong: "Close 30% More Deals -- Free CRM Trial"

**CTA in description:** Always end a description with a clear action. "Start free trial," "Get a quote today," "Book your demo."

### Example: Google Search Ad

**Situation:** SaaS project management tool, targeting "project management software"

```
H1: Manage Projects in One Place (29 chars)
H2: No More Missed Deadlines (24 chars)
H3: Free 30-Day Trial. No Card. (29 chars)
H4: See Why 12,000 Teams Chose Us (30 chars)
H5: Built for Teams Under 50 (25 chars)

D1: Stop juggling spreadsheets and Slack. One dashboard, every deadline. Start free. (84 chars)
D2: Rated 4.8 on G2 by 3,200 teams. Set up in under 10 minutes. Try it free for 30 days. (88 chars)
```

## Meta / Facebook Ads

### Character limits

| Field | Limit | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Primary text | 125 chars visible; 1,000 chars max | Text above the image/video. Only first 125 show before "See more" |
| Headline | 40 characters | Bold text below the image/video |
| Link description | 30 characters | Appears below the headline; not shown on all placements |
| Image | No text limit | But avoid text-heavy images (reduces reach) |

### Writing patterns for social ads

Meta ads interrupt. The reader was not searching for your product. You must earn their attention before making any claim.

**Hook in the first line:** The primary text must hook in the first sentence. Everything after "See more" is invisible to readers who don't click.

**Pattern: Hook + Problem + Solution + CTA**
```
[Surprising claim or relatable scenario -- 1-2 sentences]
[Name the problem with specificity]
[Introduce the product as the fix]
[CTA -- one action]
```

**Emotional before rational:** Social scrollers respond to recognition before they respond to features. Name their situation first.

### Example: Meta Ad

**Situation:** Freelance invoicing tool targeting self-employed designers

```
Primary text (hook visible before "See more"):
Most designers spend 4 hours a week on admin they hate.

[Full primary text if reader clicks "See more":]
Most designers spend 4 hours a week on admin they hate.
Chasing invoices. Reconciling payments. Formatting proposals from scratch.
Wave cuts that down to 20 minutes. Send an invoice in 60 seconds, get paid directly, and see everything in one dashboard built for one-person businesses.

Headline: Invoice in 60 Seconds. Get Paid. (39 chars)

Link description: Free for freelancers. (21 chars)
```

## LinkedIn Ads

### Character limits (Sponsored Content)

| Field | Limit | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introductory text | 150 chars visible; 600 chars max | Text above the ad creative. First 150 show in feed. |
| Headline | 70 characters | Text on the ad card below the image |
| Description | 100 characters | Appears below the headline on the card |
| CTA button | Preset options | Choose from: Apply, Download, Get Quote, Learn More, Register, Sign Up, Subscribe, Try Free, Visit Website |

### Writing patterns for LinkedIn ads

LinkedIn audiences are professional and skeptical of hype. They respond to specificity, credibility signals, and practical value. Outcomes beat claims; data beats adjectives.

**B2B framing:** Speak to the business problem and the professional consequence.

**Pattern: Result + Proof + CTA**
```
Introductory text: [Specific result] [Who achieved it or how]
Headline: [Value claim or offer]
Description: [Specificity or urgency]
```

**Avoid:** Buzzwords (innovative, cutting-edge, game-changing), vague superlatives ("the best platform"), and anything that sounds like a press release.

### Example: LinkedIn Sponsored Content Ad

**Situation:** Sales enablement platform targeting VP Sales

```
Introductory text (150 chars visible):
Enterprise sales teams using Highspot close deals 28% faster. Here's what they changed. (88 chars)

Headline: Cut Sales Cycle by 28% -- See How (34 chars)

Description: Free 30-day pilot for teams of 10+. (36 chars)

CTA button: Try Free
```

## Ad Type Patterns

| Ad type | Platform | Primary goal | Copy approach |
|---------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Search | Google | Capture intent | Mirror the query, lead with benefit, close with CTA |
| Display | Google | Build awareness | Attention-grabbing visual hook, short benefit, brand |
| Social feed | Meta, LinkedIn | Interrupt and engage | Strong hook, relatable problem, solution, CTA |
| Retargeting | Meta, LinkedIn | Convert warm traffic | Reference their prior action, overcome the objection, close |
| Story / Reel | Meta | Drive action fast | Hook in 1 second, benefit in 3, CTA in 5 |

Retargeting audiences did not convert. Address the most likely objection (price, trust, or timing) directly.

> "Still thinking about [Product]? Here's what 900 customers said after their first month." (trust)
> "Your free trial ends in 3 days. Everything you've set up stays -- if you upgrade." (timing + loss aversion)

---

## Universal Ad Copy Rules

- **One ad, one job.** Each ad drives one action. Multiple CTAs in one ad split attention and reduce conversion.
- **Count before you draft.** Hit character limits exactly; do not approximate. A headline at 31 characters will not serve.
- **Test headlines in isolation.** Because platforms show headlines in combinations you do not control, each headline must make sense without the others.
- **Match the landing page.** The headline on your ad and the headline on your landing page should echo each other. Mismatches increase bounce rate.
